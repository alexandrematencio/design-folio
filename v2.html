Hi, I’m Alex.

Professionally, I am a digital and print designer, photographer and art director, with a Bachelor’s in Project Management (ECV Digital, Paris, 2018). I run the AAXLO agency.

Outside of work I mostly try to understand how minds work, alone and in crowds. Several forms of synesthesia make me see ideas as structures, and I have spent twenty years learning to put those into words, with mixed results. What I worked out alone about consciousness and group dynamics sits closer to the research than I expected. Psychologists I know keep telling me to write it down. I am trying.

The same curiosity goes where it wants. Six years of daily economics and five on the markets, crypto included, gave me a decent feel for reading a market by the mood of its crowd. I can talk high finance, geopolitics, French politics, physics or evolutionary science for hours, though my ADHD picks the hyperfocus, not me. If your problem has people in it, I am happy to think it through with you.
